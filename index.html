Safety & Privacy

WoundWise is a prototype application designed to help users document their wound healing process. It is not a medical device. It cannot diagnose conditions, suggest treatments, or determine if a wound is infected.

Any photographs or data you upload are stored securely for your personal reference. We preserve your existing clinical logic and do not use your personal images to train AI models without consent. You have the ability to delete your records at any time. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice.
